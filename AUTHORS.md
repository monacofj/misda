### History

MISDA (Maximal Independent Structural Dimensionality Analysis) method was
originally developed in 2025-26 as as part of the Master's Thesis of Cesar 
Henrique de Souza, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Monaco F. J., at the 
Institute of Mathematical and Computer Sciences (ICMC), University of São Paulo, 
Brazil.
